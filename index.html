04 / How I work

A disciplined loop for high-stakes systems.

I bring a product and game-design process that keeps a complex experience plausible, repeatable, controllable and open to evidence-driven improvement.

  1. 01
    Frame the problem

    Clarify the user, operational or player objective; identify constraints, desired decisions, success conditions and the question the experience needs to answer.

  2. 02
    Design the system

    Define scenarios, rules, objectives, content, flows, reward or feedback structures, timelines and testable acceptance criteria.

  3. 03
    Prototype and rehearse

    Use prototypes, playtests, implementation reviews and troubleshooting to uncover ambiguity before a wider release or facilitated session.

  4. 04
    Run, observe, debrief

    Capture performance signals, participant feedback, errors and friction points so the outcome can feed back into design, content and technical configuration.

  5. 05
    Improve the reusable base

    Turn what was learned into clearer documentation, configurations, processes and content that can be repeated and scaled without losing fidelity.
